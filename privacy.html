Privacy Information Notice

This privacy policy outlines how personal information submitted voluntarily through this website template is collected and handled.

1. Information Collected Voluntarily

This static website collects personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, and message content) only when you voluntarily submit it through the contact form on the Contact Page.

2. Form Provider (Formspree)

Contact form submissions on this website are transmitted and processed using Formspree, an external form handling service. Submissions are delivered directly to the designated email account configured by the site administrator. Please review Formspree's official privacy policy for details on their data processing infrastructure.

3. Use of Information

Information submitted via the contact form is used solely for responding to your inquiry, scheduling consultations, or addressing academic collaboration requests. Information is never sold, rented, or distributed to unauthorized third parties.

4. Confidential Medical & Clinical Information

Please note that general web inquiry forms are not intended for transmitting urgent or highly sensitive medical information. Highly confidential clinical details should only be discussed directly during a formal consultation.

5. Cookies & Tracking

This static portfolio website does not utilize invasive tracking cookies or targeted advertising scripts. Standard web server logs may temporarily capture IP addresses for security and performance diagnostic purposes.
